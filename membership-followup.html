Membership renewal: January 1, 2027

Beginning January 1, 2027, membership renewals will no longer have a grace period. Member renewal dates and unit renewal dates are separate. Review members due to renew in Unit-Level Detail.

Review Key 3 coverage and SYT

Source: Units → Missing Key3, matching the Training Dive workbook queue. This source flag is separate from the training-derived Unit Key 3 Coverage definition. A flag calls for review; it does not establish that a unit will fail to renew.

Zero Total Youth

Source: Units → Total Youth = 0. Zero is a recorded count, not missing data. A forming unit or source discrepancy may need a different response from an established unit with no youth.

Unit Renewal Readiness

Source: Renewal Prep → Fix before Renewal and R (registration), S (SYT), N (missing holder), and Youth issue flags. Role requirements vary by program; unflagged roles are not independently certified. No holder names or member IDs are included here.

Unit Renewal Calendar

Source: Units → Renewal. Every matching unit is counted once, including dates outside the largest renewal cohorts and dates not recorded. These are unit renewal dates, not member expiration or projected drop dates.
