Source and method

Counts are distinct new Youth Member IDs per unit within the inclusive reporting period. Unit totals are summed as reported; this is not a council-wide deduplicated person count. The newest dated workbook for each program is selected together during the daily dashboard refresh.

Flagged joins are included in totals. Missing unit IDs and chartered organizations are unavailable, not zero. All charts, counts, table rows and CSV exports follow the current filters.
