Troop leadership readiness

Scoutmasters and Assistant Scoutmasters require IOLS to be position-trained, and at least one attending leader must have current Hazardous Weather training.

This page treats a Troop as roster-ready when at least one direct-contact leader has no outstanding S11 IOLS requirement and at least one has current Hazardous Weather training. It is not campout approval.
