Source and definitions

Daily Movo council and district aggregates for the displayed renewal cohorts. Source updated is the Google Sheet’s last modification time; the source does not supply a separate report-generation or observation timestamp. The page retains the latest validated daily source snapshots.

Renewal rate = renewed ÷ members due. Outstanding = members due − renewed, including members reported as said no. A zero denominator is unavailable. Adult/youth renewals and decline reasons display only where supplied by the source. These are renewal progress measures, not annual retention. Counts are source-reported member aggregates; person-level deduplication and concurrent registrations cannot be independently checked from this sheet.

This source has no program-type breakdown. The page uses its own cohort and district filters. Outreach timing covers both cohorts and follows only the district filter; the renewal detail, decline reasons, history and CSV follow both filters.
