Source and definitions

Daily Movo outreach aggregates by district and for the included council population. The source’s Council total refers to its Movo extract, not all council members due to renew. Source updated is the Google Sheet’s last modification time; the source does not supply a separate report-generation or observation timestamp. The page retains the latest validated daily source snapshots.

Renewal rate (Movo) = renewed in the extract ÷ members due in that extract. Outstanding (Movo) = extract members due − extract members renewed, including members reported as said no. A council-wide renewal denominator is not supplied by this source. A zero denominator is unavailable. Adult/youth renewals and decline reasons display only where supplied by the source. These are renewal progress measures, not annual retention. Counts are source-reported member aggregates; person-level deduplication and concurrent registrations cannot be independently checked from this sheet.

On October 31, 2026, Movo clarified that its performance dashboard counts members it has messaged. December outreach had not started at that time; December members appeared through households contacted about November renewals. This is dated context, not a current campaign-status indicator. The extract separately retains its Not yet messaged records.

This source has no program-type breakdown. The page uses its own cohort and district filters. Outreach timing covers both cohorts and follows only the district filter; the renewal detail, decline reasons, history and CSV follow both filters.
